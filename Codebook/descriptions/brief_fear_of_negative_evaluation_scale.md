The **Brief Fear of Negative Evaluation Scale** is a 12 item self-report questionnaire that measures a negative attitudes towards self. Items are rated on a 5 point Likert Scale ( 0- Not at all characteristic of me; 4- Extremely characteristic of me)

**Key Variables**

`bfnes_sum_score`- Sum score of items

`bfnes_total`- Sum score of items (Powered Specific)
