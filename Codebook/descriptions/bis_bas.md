The **Behavioral Approach Systems/ Behavioral Inhibition System (BIS/BAS)** scales are a 24-item self-report questionnaire that measures an individuals differences in two systems that regulate a persons motivation to either move or be aversive to a goal. Each item is rated on a 4-point likert scale (1-very true to me; 4- very false to me)

**Scoring**
