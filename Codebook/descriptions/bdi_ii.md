The **Beck Depression Inventory-II** is a 21-item self report questionnaire that measures severity of depression symptoms. Each time is rated on a 4 point likert scale from 0 to 3.

**Scoring**

A sum score is taken with higher scores indicating higher severity.

Key Items

`bdi_total_score_weighted_sum` - Total weighted sum of items

`bdi_total_score- sum score`
