The **Athlete's Relationship with Training Scale** is a 15-item self report questionnaire that assesses unhealthy training behaviors and beliefs in athletes.

**Scoring**

Four subscales are calculated by taking sum scores.

Affect-Driven Training Subscale: `art_affective_driven'`

Training Amount Score Subscale: `art_training_amount`

Training Against Medical Advice Subscale: `art_against_med_advice`

Body Dissatisfaction Subscale: `art_body_dissatisfy`
