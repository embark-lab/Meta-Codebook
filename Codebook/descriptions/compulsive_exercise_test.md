The **Compulsive Exercise Test i**s a 24-item self-report questionnaire that assesses maladaptive exercise. Each item is rated on a 6-point Likert scale (0 = Never True; 5 = Always True). There are five sub scales: Avoidance and Rule-Driven Behavior; Weight Control Exercise; Mood Improvement; Lack of Exercise Enjoyment; Exercise Rigidity.

**Key Variables**

`cet_total_score`- Total sum score

`cet_avoid_subscale` - CET Avoid Subscale

`cet_wtcontrol_subscale`- CET Weight Control Subscale

`cet_mood_subscale` - CET Mood Subscale
