The **Difficulties in Emotion Regulation Scale i**s a 36-item self-report questionnaire that measures emotional regulation. Items are rated on a 5-point Likert scale (0- Almost Never, 4- Always).

**Scoring**

There are six subscales that are calculated by adding the sum of items:

Nonacceptance of Emotional Responses

Difficulty engaging in goal-directed behavior

Impulse Control Difficulties

Lack of Emotional Awareness

Limited access to emotion regulation strategies

Lack of Emotional Clarity
